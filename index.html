How to Play
Defend your city from waves of raiders.
  • A wave of raiders is coming for your city. Take a look at what's headed your way before you act.
  • Pick a unit on the left, then click your side of the map to place it. Each unit costs Population, so spend it wisely.
  • Once you start the battle, it plays out on its own — no further input from you. Just watch how your placement holds up.
  • Clear a level to earn Gold for permanent Upgrades and collect Items dropped by raiders.
  • High ground gives a damage bonus, and cliffs block melee attacks — use the terrain.
  • If your defenders are wiped out, your City Health takes damage. Get it to 0 and the run ends.
